Congratulations! You have received an internship or job offer. But what’s next?

Beyond celebrating, you will need to evaluate the offer, understand the contract terms, assess your motivators, and decide how to accept or decline professionally. This workshop helps you navigate these decisions with confidence and integrity.

Expert trainer Clement Ong will guide you through case studies, breakout discussions, and live feedback sessions to help you handle job offers and salary negotiations effectively.

Participants Will Learn:

  • Identify your motivators and evaluate employment contracts and remuneration packages.
  • Professionally and ethically manage multiple job offers (Dos and Don’ts).
  • Apply negotiation strategies and respond confidently to salary-related questions.
  • Understand key negotiation techniques used in the workplace.
  • Accept or decline offers gracefully and professionally.
  • Handle rejections with composure and confidence.

Who Should Attend:

  • Students applying for or currently receiving internship or full-time job offers.

This is a post-event workshop for NUS Career Fest 2026.

Speaker(s)

Clement Ong
Career Coach & Trainer

Clement Ong, the Founder and Director of Lighthouse Pte Ltd, has over 20 years of experience in providing quality and effective career management, career transition/outplacement and executive coaching programs to major MNCs and large listed organisations from a wide range of industries. He works with clients ranging from senior management to support-level employees locally and in the region.

He is a Certified Career Practitioner (CCP) awarded by Workforce Singapore. His designation of Career Management Fellow (CMF) is awarded by the Institute of Career Certification International (ICCI), USA. Clement facilitates career skills workshops with the major universities in Singapore.

Calling all students! Want to make your communication skills more visible, observable, and employability-relevant?

Join CommCircle.
A small group peer practice circle where you can try, refine, and build confidence in workplace communication in a supportive, low-stakes environment. You’re welcome to experiment, make mistakes, and learn through practice and feedback. CommCircle enables students to develop and demonstrate communication competencies valued by employers through practice, feedback, and real-world communication scenarios.

What to expect

In each session, trained CommPeers (top performers from CommsLab, CFG1600/S) will facilitate practice and provide structured, actionable feedback to help you develop, demonstrate, and articulate communication competencies that employers value.

You can work on areas such as:

  • Speaking and interpersonal communication
  • Influencing and persuasion
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Structured thinking and clear articulation
  • Executive presence
  • Managing communication anxiety in high-stakes situations

What you need to prepare

To benefit fully from the session, please come prepared with a piece of communication you want to practise and get feedback on. You have flexibility in what you bring. Examples include:

  • Career and employer pitches (based on real job descriptions)
  • Stakeholder presentations
  • Internship reflections
  • Influencing without authority

Sessions run every Wednesday of even weeks.

Registration

Sign up via this MSForm. Slots are first-come, first-served, and limited to keep group sizes small and feedback high quality. If you’re allocated a slot, CommPeers will send you a confirmation email with the session details.

✅Mr. Lim Lek Tong, CA (Singapore) | General Manager, Finance & Treasury, Swire Bulk
Twenty years across corporate finance, treasury, financial planning and business transformation in the maritime sector

✅Ms. Vivian Neo, CA (Singapore) | Audit & Assurance Partner, Deloitte & Touche LLP
A career auditing shipping companies in Singapore and New York

✅Moderated by Dr Wang Jiwei, Professor of Accounting (Practice) and Associate Dean at Singapore Management University, and a member of the ISCA Financial Reporting Committee.

Synopsis:

We are happy to share that NTU, EDB and SSIA will be hosting an upcoming PACE Forward Event on The Next-Gen Semiconductor Workforce on 20 Oct 2026 (Tuesday). This gathering offers a comprehensive look at the road ahead for engineering specialists, and where NTU will also share more on the recently launched NTU FlexiMasters in Integrated Circuit Design for students and practicsing engineers to upgrade their technical toolkit.

With the microelectronics and chip-manufacturing sector poised for massive international growth over the remainder of the decade, our local ecosystem is scaling its capabilities at an unprecedented rate. This rapid expansion brings a pressing need for deep technical expertise, particularly in advanced integrated circuit (IC) creation.

The details for the PACE Forward Event are as follows:

When: Tuesday, 20 October 2026 | 6PM – 9PM

Where: EDB Office (Raffles City Tower, Level 21)

What We Will Cover

The Big Picture Outlook: Hear directly from EDB regarding how our nation plans to secure its competitive edge and drive future hardware innovation.

The Recruitment Pulse: SSIA will share what hiring managers are prioritising right now, alongside fresh strategies for nurturing technical leaders.

Bridging the Knowledge Gap: Assoc Prof Kim Tae Hyoung will introduce a brand-new academic pathway — the NTU FlexiMasters in Integrated Circuit Design. You will discover how this modular learning approach translates academic principles into immediate, practical utility for top-tier tech companies.

Interactive Dialogue: Engage in a live exchange with industry veterans and leaders, guided by Prof Kim, discussing the realities of thriving in this fast-paced discipline.

This is a valuable chance to learn exactly which competencies leading hardware firms value most and how you can equip yourself to meet those needs.
