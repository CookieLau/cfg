NUS Biomedical Sciences Interdisciplinary Industry Academy Webinar Series 2026-10-07 2026-10-07 N/A 🔗 Education

From Mechanistic Bench Science to Global Regulation: Advancing Animal-Free Safety Assessment via New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)As global regulations evolve, the cosmetic industry has acted as a pioneer in eliminating animal testing. The transition toward animal-free safety assessment represents one of the most significant shifts in modern biomedical
and industrial science.

This session explores the interdisciplinary journey and the technological transition of
replacing traditional in vivo toxicology—specifically the Draize test for skin and eye irritation assessment—with robust, science-based New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). Dr. Alépée will discuss the engineering and biological characterization of 3D Reconstructed Human Epidermis (RhE) models and their integration into sophisticated testing toolboxes. The presentation will detail the evolution of Integrated Approaches to Testing and Assessment (IATA) and Defined Approaches (DA), illustrating how the rule-based integration of physico-chemical properties and in vitro data can provide higher predictive accuracy than legacy animal models. Through case studies on liquids, solid chemicals, and surfactants, this talk demonstrates the successful translation of academic research into standardized OECD Test Guidelines, ensuring consumer safety through the lens of the 3Rs and Next-Generation Risk Assessment
(NGRA) frameworks.

Dr. Nathalie Alépée is a distinguished expert in molecular biology and investigative toxicology with nearly 30 years of experience across the pharmaceutical and cosmetic sectors. Following her PhD research at CNRS and a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Cambridge, she spent a decade at Pfizer before joining L’Oréal R&I in 2007. Her career is defined by her role as a scientific bridge-builder, translating advanced mechanistic research into validated New Approach Methodologies (NAMs). With an H-index of 35 and over 3,300 citations, Dr. Alépée has authored approximately 100 peer reviewed papers and has been instrumental in the international adoption of over 15 OECD Test Guidelines (including TG 431, 439, and 492B). As the French Representative at the OECD and a key contributor to the ICCS and EPAA, she leads
interdisciplinary efforts to implement animal-free safety assessments globally.

This webinar series showcases real-world case studies from industry projects to help students understand current challenges and how they are addressed in practice. By highlighting industry practices, decision-making processes, and the application of both technical expertise and professional competencies, the series aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations.

  • Internship reflections
  • Influencing without authority

Synopsis:

AI agents can now act autonomously — executing tasks, calling tools, accessing data, and even browsing the internet without constant human oversight. But this autonomy comes with real risk: in mid-2026, an OpenAI internal security assessment saw its own model-based agents bypass isolation controls and gain unauthorized access to Hugging Face's production infrastructure, logging roughly 17,600 actions before the breach was detected and contained.
