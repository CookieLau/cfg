1. Recognise…

thinking patterns that get you down
when you need help and where to get it

2. Rewire…
your thoughts – experiencing mindfulness for resilience
to regulate difficult emotions

3. Ready, Go!
Actionable plans to move forward.

Calling all students! Want to make your communication skills more visible, observable, and employability-relevant?

Join CommCircle.
A small group peer practice circle where you can try, refine, and build confidence in workplace communication in a supportive, low-stakes environment. You’re welcome to experiment, make mistakes, and learn through practice and feedback. CommCircle enables students to develop and demonstrate communication competencies valued by employers through practice, feedback, and real-world communication scenarios.

What to expect

In each session, trained CommPeers (top performers from CommsLab, CFG1600/S) will facilitate practice and provide structured, actionable feedback to help you develop, demonstrate, and articulate communication competencies that employers value.

You can work on areas such as:

  • Speaking and interpersonal communication
  • Influencing and persuasion
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Structured thinking and clear articulation
  • Executive presence
  • Managing communication anxiety in high-stakes situations

What you need to prepare

To benefit fully from the session, please come prepared with a piece of communication you want to practise and get feedback on. You have flexibility in what you bring. Examples include:

  • Career and employer pitches (based on real job descriptions)
  • Stakeholder presentations
  • Internship reflections
  • Influencing without authority

Sessions run every Wednesday of even weeks.

Registration

Sign up via this MSForm. Slots are first-come, first-served, and limited to keep group sizes small and feedback high quality. If you’re allocated a slot, CommPeers will send you a confirmation email with the session details.

Trustworthy Agentic AI at Work – Skills & Mindsets for an Autonomous World, presented by Cloudsine 2026-10-20 2026-10-20 N/A 🔗 Information Communications Technology

College of Design & Engineering

Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences

NUS Business School

School of Computing

Faculty of Science

Faculty of Law

 All students

Featuring:

Matthias Chin, Founder and CEO

Synopsis:

AI agents can now act autonomously — executing tasks, calling tools, accessing data, and even browsing the internet without constant human oversight. But this autonomy comes with real risk: in mid-2026, an OpenAI internal security assessment saw its own model-based agents bypass isolation controls and gain unauthorized access to Hugging Face's production infrastructure, logging roughly 17,600 actions before the breach was detected and contained. Incidents like this raise a critical question for every organization deploying agentic AI: how do you ensure trustworthiness — not just of the model, but of every action it takes, every tool it calls, and every piece of data it touches? Drawing on experience building security for autonomous AI systems used by enterprises and governments across the region, this talk breaks down the skills and mindsets — building harness, evaluation, governance thinking, and guardrails — that will define careers in a workplace where AI agents increasingly act on our behalf.
