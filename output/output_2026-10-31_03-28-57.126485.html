Calling all students! Want to make your communication skills more visible, observable, and employability-relevant?

Join CommCircle.
A small group peer practice circle where you can try, refine, and build confidence in workplace communication in a supportive, low-stakes environment. You’re welcome to experiment, make mistakes, and learn through practice and feedback. CommCircle enables students to develop and demonstrate communication competencies valued by employers through practice, feedback, and real-world communication scenarios.

What to expect

In each session, trained CommPeers (top performers from CommsLab, CFG1600/S) will facilitate practice and provide structured, actionable feedback to help you develop, demonstrate, and articulate communication competencies that employers value.

You can work on areas such as:

  • Speaking and interpersonal communication
  • Influencing and persuasion
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Structured thinking and clear articulation
  • Executive presence
  • Managing communication anxiety in high-stakes situations

What you need to prepare

To benefit fully from the session, please come prepared with a piece of communication you want to practise and get feedback on. You have flexibility in what you bring. Examples include:

  • Career and employer pitches (based on real job descriptions)
  • Stakeholder presentations
  • Internship reflections
  • Influencing without authority

Sessions run every Wednesday of even weeks.

Registration

Sign up via this MSForm. Slots are first-come, first-served, and limited to keep group sizes small and feedback high quality. If you’re allocated a slot, CommPeers will send you a confirmation email with the session details.

Which master’s is right for you? Ask the people who lead them.
From 10 to 13 November, the College of Design and Engineering, NUS is hosting 12 live online info sessions for the August 2027 intake, one per department or centre, covering all 28 of our master’s programmes.

Why join
Choosing a master’s programme is a significant decision, with each programme offering distinct opportunities to deepen expertise, broaden perspectives and advance your career. Whether you are looking to build on your knowledge in an established discipline or explore new possibilities through a multidisciplinary approach, the key is finding a programme that aligns with your aspirations. Equally important is understanding how each programme is evolving in response to emerging technologies such as AI, and how these developments are being integrated into your chosen field.

What you’ll hear
Programme Directors and faculty will cover:

  • What you’ll study and how the curriculum is structured
  • Where graduates go on to work
  • What makes a strong application

Each session ends with a live Q&A, so bring your questions.

Who should attend

  • International students considering postgraduate study in Singapore, a leading hub for AI and innovation in Asia
  • Undergraduates in Year 2 or above from engineering, science, architecture, design, business or a related field, considering a master’s for August 2027 or beyond

  • Working professionals looking to deepen their expertise or change direction

💻Online via Zoom, 60 minutes per session
